Every step fills your world

1. Set the progress. Move a quest from 0 to 100%. Its artwork fills with color, and that progress travels up its branches.

2. Give big tasks their weight. Small, medium, and large tasks count as 1, 3, and 5. Branches have equal weight by default; adjust them in Edit branches.

3. Build a balanced Life score. Each active life area contributes equally by default. Empty branches are left out. Tasks attached directly to a parent form one extra group.

4. Earn, and keep, your achievements. XP follows each task’s highest-ever progress. Undoing and redoing the same task gives no extra XP. Badges stay earned even when you add new tasks.

5. Feed your attributes. Each quest contributes to Heart, Soul, Smile, or Strength. These are task-completion meters, not measures of your worth or health.
